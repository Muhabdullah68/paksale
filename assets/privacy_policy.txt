1. Terms & Conditions / Terms of Use

This is the main legal agreement between  app and users.

The app is a marketplace platform, not the seller or buyer.

The platform provides technology that allows users to publish listings, communicate, and arrange transactions. Unless specifically stated otherwise, the platform is not the owner, seller, buyer, agent, broker, guarantor, or manufacturer of listed products or services.
Users must:
Provide accurate information.
Use their own mobile number.
Not create fake accounts.
Not impersonate another person.
Not upload stolen images or copyrighted material.
Not mislead other users.
Not manipulate ratings or reviews.
Not use the platform for fraud or illegal activity.

Required:

Business information
CNIC or other legally appropriate verification through a secure process

3. Your Best Trust System for Pakistan

I recommend this structure:

The phone number has been verified by OTP.

🔵 Identity Verified

The platform has completed an appropriate identity-verification process.

🟡 Business Verified

Business information has been checked.

⭐ Trusted Seller

The seller has:

Completed multiple transactions
Received positive feedback
Avoided serious complaints
Maintained a good account history
⚠️ New Seller

A new account should clearly display:

New Seller — Trade Carefully

This is much better than pretending that every user is equally trustworthy.

6. Transaction Policy

Your policy should make clear:

The platform does not automatically guarantee the quality, ownership, authenticity, condition, legality, or delivery of every item listed by a user.

However, if you later introduce escrow, protected payments, delivery, or a managed marketplace, you should create separate rules for those services.

For a high-value item, your app can display:

For your protection, inspect the item before completing payment.

Your system should discourage users from sending money merely because a seller sends a screenshot or makes an urgent demand.

10. Reviews & Ratings Policy

To prevent fake reviews:

Only allow a review when:
A transaction or meaningful interaction has occurred, or
The platform has a reasonable basis to allow the review.
Prohibit:
Paying for fake reviews
Threatening someone to change a review
Creating multiple accounts to manipulate ratings
Reviewing your own listing

Your system should preserve both:

Seller Rating

and

Buyer Rating

This is especially useful for Pakistani marketplaces.

11. Account Suspension & Termination Policy

Your app should have the right to:

Remove listings
Restrict accounts
Suspend accounts
Permanently ban accounts
Remove fraudulent content
Preserve relevant records for investigations

But you should also provide a basic appeal process:

A user may appeal an account action through customer support. The platform may request additional information before reviewing the appeal.

12. Children & Age Policy

I recommend:

Users must be at least 18 years old to independently create listings or conduct marketplace transactions, unless the service is specifically designed for younger users with appropriate legal safeguards.

This is especially important because buying and selling, identity verification, payments, and private communications can create additional risks for minors.

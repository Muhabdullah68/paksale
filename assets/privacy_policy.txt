Privacy Policy

PakSale

Effective Date: September 2026

Thank you for using PakSale. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our mobile application, why we collect it, how it is used, shared, and protected, and the choices you have regarding your information. By creating an account or using PakSale, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

PakSale is operated from Pakistan as a local marketplace platform. Our practices reflect Pakistan's developing data governance framework and the principle of data minimization: we collect only what we need and we protect it with reasonable care.

1. Who We Are

PakSale is a peer-to-peer marketplace platform that allows individual and business users across Pakistan to list, discover, discuss, and complete transactions for second-hand and new goods, vehicles, property, services, and community postings. We are not the seller, buyer, owner, agent, broker, or guarantor of any item listed through the app. We provide the technology, hosting, and trust tools that make these connections possible.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information depending on how you use PakSale.

Account and profile information. When you sign up we ask for your Pakistani mobile number, your display name, and your city or local area. You may optionally add an email address, a profile photograph, and a short bio. For sellers who choose a verified or business tier we may collect additional details including business name, business contact information, and in appropriate cases an identification reference number sufficient to complete a secure verification process. We do not publish complete identity numbers or full document scans on any public profile.

Listing and marketplace content. When you publish a listing we store the title, description, price, photographs or video, category, condition, location tag, and any custom attributes you provide. We also store records of listings you save or mark as a favorite.

Messages and communications. Chat messages between buyers and sellers are stored on our servers so that conversations remain available across devices and so that either party or our support team can refer back to them in the event of a dispute, scam report, or safety investigation. Messages are not sold or used for advertising purposes.

Device and technical information. Our app automatically collects limited technical information including your device model, operating system version, app version, approximate connection type, Internet protocol address, and error logs. This information helps us diagnose crashes, maintain compatibility, and detect suspicious activity that may indicate abuse of the platform.

Camera and media files. If you grant the camera permission we access your camera only when you choose to take a photograph for a listing or to send a photograph in chat. We do not access your camera in the background. Photographs you take within the app are uploaded only when you press submit. You may also upload existing images from your media library; we access those files only for the purpose of attaching them to your listing or message.

Location. PakSale uses city and area-level location information to help buyers find sellers nearby. We do not ask for continuous background location and we do not publish any user's exact residential address. Public listings show a locality tag such as the name of a neighborhood, town, or city. Users who meet in person can share a precise meeting point through private chat if both parties agree.

Usage and analytics. We collect basic usage data such as the screens you visit, the searches you perform, listing views, and the general time of day you use the app. This helps us understand which categories are popular and where we should focus product improvements. Analytics are summarized where possible and individual behavior records are not sold to external marketers.

3. Why We Collect Information

We use your information only for specific, practical purposes.

To create and maintain your account. Your mobile number is the primary account key and is verified through a one-time SMS code so that we can reduce fake accounts and impersonation.

To operate the marketplace. Listing details, profile details, and city-level location are required so that buyers and sellers can find each other, communicate, and safely complete trades.

To verify accounts and build trust signals. PakSale offers graduated verification levels: basic mobile-verified accounts, optionally identity-verified accounts, and business-verified accounts. Each level produces a visible trust badge that helps other users trade with greater confidence.

To prevent fraud, scams, and prohibited activity. We scan patterns of behavior including rapid account creation, unusual messaging patterns, reports from users, and content matches against our prohibited items list. These checks help us identify and remove fake payment confirmations, advance-fee scams, impersonation accounts, counterfeit listings, and other harmful content before it reaches other users.

To provide customer support and resolve disputes. When a user contacts our support team or opens a dispute we review the account history, relevant listings, and related chat history so that we can understand both sides and reach a fair outcome.

To improve the app. Aggregated and de-identified usage data informs our decisions about new features, performance optimizations, and interface adjustments.

To comply with legal obligations. We may retain and share records when we are lawfully required to do so by a Pakistani court order, a competent regulator, or a recognized law enforcement authority operating with proper procedure.

4. How We Share Information

We treat your information as confidential and we do not sell it. The following circumstances describe the limited cases in which information may be shared.

With other marketplace users. Any information you place on a public listing, including your public display name, profile photograph, listing images, city-level location, and listing description is visible to other users of the app who browse that category or run a related search. Your mobile number is never published publicly on a listing page; contact between users takes place through our in-app chat, and users may choose to share further contact details only by their own decision inside a private conversation.

With service providers that run parts of the platform. We use established cloud infrastructure and service providers for hosting, database operation, file storage, push notifications, SMS delivery, image processing, and mobile analytics. These providers process data only on our written instructions and under reasonable security obligations.

With fraud and safety partners. Where we judge it necessary to protect users from financial loss or physical harm we may share limited account details with recognized fraud-prevention services or with authorities investigating criminal activity.

With law enforcement and regulators. We respond to requests for account records, chat records, or listing records when the request is in writing, identifies a lawful basis for disclosure, and references the specific account or matter under investigation. We do not volunteer blanket access to our user database.

In connection with a business transfer. If PakSale or substantially all of its assets are acquired by another company, or if a restructuring takes place, account records would be among the transferred assets and would continue to be handled under a privacy policy with equivalent protections.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as there is a practical or legal reason to keep it. Active account information is retained for as long as your account remains open. Listing history and closed chat threads are retained for a reasonable period after a listing closes so that users can refer back to past transactions and so that our team can review historical reports.

If you delete your account we remove your profile, listings, and personal contact details from public view within a short period, and we delete the underlying personal records from our active databases as soon as practical, subject to our need to retain limited accounting, dispute, and law-enforcement records for the additional periods required by applicable Pakistani law and prudent record-keeping practice.

6. Security Measures

No mobile app or online service can guarantee absolute security, but we apply reasonable safeguards. Personal information is stored on access-controlled servers with encrypted storage volumes, connections between our app and our servers use encrypted transport, sensitive administrative actions require internal approval, and access to user records by our own staff is granted only on a need-to-know basis for support, safety, or engineering reasons. Camera and location permissions are requested only at the point the feature is used and may be revoked at any time through your device settings.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Update or correct your profile. You may change your display name, email address, profile photograph, bio, and city directly from the in-app profile screen at any time.

Remove listings or saved items. Any listing you published can be marked as sold, archived, or deleted through the listing menu, and items you saved to favorites can be removed individually.

Manage permissions. You can withdraw the camera permission through your mobile device settings. Withdrawing permission will prevent you from taking new photographs within the app but will not affect existing listings or other app features. You can also disable notifications through system settings.

Opt out of analytics where available. You may request that we stop collecting non-essential usage analytics on your account by writing to our support address. Essential records required for account security, fraud detection, and legal compliance cannot be opted out of.

Request deletion of your account. You may request full account deletion through the account settings screen or by writing to our support team. We process deletion requests promptly and we send you a confirmation when the main account record is closed. As noted above, a small set of records may be retained in our restricted archives for legal or dispute-related reasons before permanent deletion.

Access the information we hold about you. You may request a written summary of the personal information we hold in relation to your account by writing to our support address. We aim to respond to access, correction, and deletion requests within a reasonable period appropriate to the size and nature of the request.

8. Children and Young Users

PakSale is a marketplace for adults to trade personal property and arrange in-person meetings. For this reason users must be at least 18 years old to create a seller account, publish listings, or conduct marketplace transactions independently. We do not knowingly collect the personal information of children under the age of 13. If you become aware that a minor has provided information directly to us please notify our support team and we will take prompt steps to remove the account and its related personal data.

9. Camera, Internet, and Other Sensitive Permissions

PakSale requests the following permissions only when the related feature is used. Internet access is required throughout the session so that listings, messages, and profile information can load from our servers. The camera permission is requested only when you tap the camera icon inside a listing draft or inside a chat conversation that is about to attach a photograph. Additional sensitive permissions such as fine location or access to contacts are not requested automatically; should such a permission become necessary in a future release it will be explained clearly at the time of the request and tied to a specific user-initiated action.

10. Third-Party Services Used in the App

PakSale uses the following standard platform services to operate. Each provider handles information according to its own public privacy policy in addition to the instructions we give as the controller of your data.

Cloud, database, file storage, push messaging, and analytics infrastructure is provided by Firebase from Google. Firebase helps us run the app, deliver one-time SMS codes, send push notifications for new messages, and collect basic stability and usage analytics. The Firebase services we use are configured to collect only the information necessary for those purposes.

Image loading and caching libraries make local, temporary copies of listing photographs on your device so that repeated browsing is faster and uses less mobile data. These cached files remain on your device until the app's local cache is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

SMS delivery providers send the short one-time verification codes that confirm ownership of your mobile number. We share only the destination number and the short code content that is required for delivery.

In the normal course of use we do not share personal data with advertising networks or marketing brokers, and we do not run behavioral retargeting campaigns across third-party websites.

11. Changes to This Policy

From time to time we may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features, a change in the law, or a clarification of our current practice. When we make material changes we will post a short notice inside the app or send you an in-app announcement so that you can review the updated text before it takes effect. Continuing to use PakSale after the updated policy takes effect means you accept the revised text. Non-material clarifications may be published without a separate notice and we encourage you to review this page from time to time.
